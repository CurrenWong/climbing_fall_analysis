3.13.3.7 Mass Damping
The “mass damping” body load applies a body force that is proportional to the linear momentum
density p = ρv, where ρ is the material density and v the velocity. An additional scale factor can
be used to control the strength of the force, f = Cp.
parameter Description default
C damping coefficient 0
