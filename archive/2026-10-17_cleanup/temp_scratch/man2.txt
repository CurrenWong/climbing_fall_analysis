 15846| FEBio uses an iterative method to solve the nonlinear finite element equations. This means that
 15847| for each time step the solution for that step is obtained by a succession of iterations, where each
 15848| iteration brings the model (hopefully) closer to the solution for that step. An iterative method

 15850| requires a convergence criterion to decide when to stop iterating and FEBio uses no less than
 15851| three criteria. (In biphasic and multiphasic problems additional convergence criteria are used for
 15852| the pressure and concentration degrees of freedom.) The convergence norm for each of these
 15853| criteria is defined by the user in the control section of the input file.
 15854| Sometimes it happens that FEBio cannot converge on a time step. We’ll take a look at some
 15855| of the most common causes in the next following sections. Also take a look at possible causes

 15858| 8.3.2.1 No loads applied
 15859| Perhaps surprisingly, when no loads are applied, FEBio will often struggle to find a solution. The

 15862| norms not exactly zero. FEBio then tries to apply the convergence criteria to these really small
 15863| norms and again due to round-off error will not be able to satisfy the convergence criteria.
 15864| FEBio can actually detect this situation. It looks at the residual norm and when this is smaller

 15872| 8.3.2.2 Convergence Tolerance Too Tight
 15873| In some cases, the default convergence tolerances are too tight and FEBio will not be able to

 15876| should only be done when no other cause can be identified for the convergence problems.
 15877| 8.3.2.3 Forcing convergence
 15878| It is possible to force a time step to converge. This can be done by interrupting the run (using
 15879| ctrl+c) and then enter the conv command at the FEBio prompt. This will force the time step to
 15880| converge and FEBio will continue with the next time step. This is not a recommended practice
 15881| as the solution may become unstable and in fact it is unlikely that FEBio will be able to converge
 15882| any of the following time steps. However, it can be useful in some scenarios. For example, if
 15883| there is no load applied in the initial time step and FEBio has trouble getting past this initial step
